The problem

When the government needs land for a highway, railway or industrial project, dozens of steps, officers, departments and documents are involved. Today they live in separate places.

Scattered records

Land records, survey data, notices and payments sit in different systems with no single view of one acquisition.

No visible next step

Officers cannot easily see who must act, what is blocking the case, or which deadline is slipping.

Citizens left in the dark

Landowners do not know their case status, when to object, or when compensation will arrive.

Weak audit trail

Who approved what, and when, is hard to reconstruct. Skipped steps go unnoticed.

The solution: one acquisition case

BHUSAMANVAY makes the case the centre of everything. Land, people, tasks, evidence, decisions, money and history all belong to the same case.

Project→Parcel→Acquisition Case→Workflow / Task→Evidence / Field→Decision / Review→Audit

What is new, and what was missing

What is missing todayWhat BHUSAMANVAY adds
Land, workflow and payment tools do not talk to each otherOne case-centric record linking parcel, task, evidence, decision and audit
Steps can be skipped or done out of orderWorkflow engine locks the next stage until the previous one is complete
Location and authority are matched by handJurisdiction routing from State → District → Taluk → Village to the responsible authority
Field data is lost without networkField officer app designed for offline capture and later sync
Citizens cannot see progressCitizen portal with plain-language status and next step
Replacing existing systems is unrealisticOrchestration, not replacement: adapters connect DILRMP/ULPIN, state land records, Bhoomi Rashi, PFMS, PM Gati Shakti
Delays found too lateRule-based bottleneck detection against SLA now; predictive intelligence planned
Weak accountabilityEvery action logged: who, what, when, case, old status → new status
